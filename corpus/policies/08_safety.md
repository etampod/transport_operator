---

## Emergency intercom

In an emergency on a train, a passenger must use the emergency intercom.

## Doors

A passenger must not open the doors between stations.

## Bicycles

Bicycles are allowed on off-peak trains. Bicycles are not allowed on trains arriving in the city centre between 07:30 and 09:30.

## Unattended luggage

Unattended luggage must be reported to station staff immediately.
