---

## Compensation thresholds

A delay under 30 minutes does not qualify for compensation. A delay of 30 to 59 minutes qualifies for compensation of 50 percent of the single ticket price. A delay of 60 minutes or more qualifies for compensation of 100 percent of the single ticket price.

## Claim window

A delay compensation claim must be submitted within 28 days of the journey.

## Announced strikes

Compensation is not paid when the delay was caused by a strike announced at least 48 hours in advance.
