## First response

Podoba Transit sends a first response to a complaint within 5 working days.

## Final response

A final response to a complaint is sent within 20 working days. The 20 working day period starts when the complaint is received.

## Ombudsman

A passenger who is not satisfied with the final response can refer the case to the Independent Travel Ombudsman.
