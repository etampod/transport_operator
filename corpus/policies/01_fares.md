title: Fares and daily caps
---

## Adult single tickets

An adult single ticket costs 2.40 EUR in zone 1. Each additional zone adds 1.20 EUR.

## Child fares

A child single ticket is half the adult single fare for the same number of zones. A child is a passenger under 16 years of age.

## Adult day cap

The adult day cap is 9.60 EUR. Once an adult has paid 9.60 EUR on single tickets in one calendar day, later single trips that same day are free.

## Senior Card

Holders of a Podoba Senior Card travel free on weekdays after 09:30 and all day on weekends.
